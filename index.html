HOW TO PLAY.

  1. One player is the judge each round. The judge rotates clockwise.
  2. Everyone else gets 10 white answer cards.
  3. The judge reveals a black prompt. Pick the funniest answer in your hand.
  4. Submissions stay anonymous until the judge picks a winner.
  5. The winning player gets 1 point. First to 10 wins.
  6. Used cards are replaced. Keep the chaos moving.

18+ humor. Play with people who know your sense of humor.
